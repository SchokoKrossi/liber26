48hLIBERfilm 2026

Make a film in 48 hours!

Write, shoot, edit and submit!
Set up your team and register now!

The participation is open to everyone. Register until July 19th, 2026.

🎬 Register your team

🎬 Join the 48h LIBERfilm 🎬

Get ready for a weekend of creativity, chaos, and cinema!

We'll kick off on Friday, July 31st at 6:00 PM at Café Engels (Herrfurthstraße 21, 12049 Berlin). At the event, we'll go over the rules, and each team will draw a random film genre to base their movie on.

From Friday at 7:00 PM to Sunday, August 2nd at 7:00 PM, you'll have exactly 48 hours to write, shoot, edit, and submit your short film.

🎥 How to Join

  • Gather a team (max. 8 people recommended).
  • Pick a fun team name.
  • Prepare your gear (phone/camera, costumes, props, etc.).
  • Register your team using the form below.
  • Mark your calendars — and get ready!

📝 The Rules

Every film must include:

  • A randomly assigned genre
  • A given character name (must be said aloud or shown clearly)
  • A given sentence
  • A given object (must be visibly included at least once)
  • One sentence in French (or in German if the film is in French)
  • Maximum length: 7 minutes
  • Must begin with: 3 seconds of black screen, followed by 4 seconds titled "48h LIBERfilm"
  • In the audio, any language is allowed — please add English subtitles. Subtitles can be added after the deadline (max. until August 5th), so you have more time for them.
  • Final file must be under 2 GB, submitted via WeTransfer, Drive link, etc.

🚫 Note: Use of AI tools (e.g. for writing, editing, image generation) is not allowed — except for subtitling, if needed.

🎉 Screening & Awards Night

All films will be screened on Sunday, August 16th at the ACUD Theatre (Veteranenstraße 21, 10119 Berlin). Everyone who participates is invited to attend. A jury will select winners in the following categories:

  • 🏆 Best Actress / Best Actor
  • 🏆 Best Use of the Prompt
  • 🏆 Best Cinematography
  • 🏆 Jury's Favorite

But above all: Have fun, create boldly, and embrace the chaos of filmmaking!

📣 Want to Join the Jury?

We're looking for jury members! If you're not participating in a team but still want to be involved, you can help select the winners. After the competition weekend, you'll receive access to all submitted films and a rating form.

Fill in the form to apply as a jury member.
